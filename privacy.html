ARC AFRICA · YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-18

This Privacy Policy explains how Arc Africa (“we”, “us” or “the Platform”) collects, uses, stores and shares personal data when you visit the website, create an account, apply for membership, submit a project, contribute to the community or contact us. We aim to handle personal data lawfully, fairly, transparently and only for clear purposes, in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name or display name, email address, account identifier, account creation and verification status, and authentication timestamps.
  • Profile data: username, profile image URL, biography and location details you choose to provide.
  • Membership application data: name, email, county/location, GitHub username, LinkedIn URL, optional portfolio, interests, experience, motivation and review outcome.
  • Contribution data: project details, links, updates, community posts, comments, ratings and saved-project actions you submit.
  • Contact data: name, email, subject and message when you contact the Platform.
  • Security and activity metadata: sign-in timestamps and session counts, plus records of account/profile changes, session starts and revocations, project submissions and edits, updates, comments, ratings, favourites, community posts and membership application or moderation actions.
  • Technical data: limited information generated by hosting, authentication and security services as needed to deliver and protect the Platform. We do not intentionally collect precise location, device sensors or unrelated browsing history.

2. How activity monitoring works

To protect the community, resolve failed submissions, maintain moderation accountability and investigate suspected misuse, the administrator can review a restricted activity feed. It records the type of action, the affected record identifier, the account identifier where available, the time and the names of fields changed. It does not copy the text of comments, private contact messages, project descriptions or application motivations into the activity log.

We do not use this feature to record keystrokes, passwords, authentication tokens, private browsing activity, screen contents or the contents of private communications. The administrator can see content you deliberately submit to public or moderation queues and messages you deliberately send through the contact form, as needed to operate the service.

3. Why we use personal data

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users and manage sessions.
  • Review membership applications and administer member access.
  • Publish and manage projects, updates, community posts, comments and ratings.
  • Respond to enquiries and send service-related notices.
  • Detect abuse, investigate errors, protect users, maintain audit trails and improve reliability.
  • Meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our Terms of Use.

4. Lawful basis and choice

Depending on the activity, processing may be based on steps you request to provide the service, your consent, our legitimate interests in platform security and community administration, or compliance with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it; withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. You may choose not to provide optional profile or project information, but some features will not work without information required for that feature.

5. Public information

Approved project listings and community contributions may be visible to the public and indexed or copied by third parties. Do not publish information you expect to remain confidential. Your membership application and account-security records are not intended to be public. Public profile fields and other content are displayed according to the feature and its visibility.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may share limited personal data with service providers that host the website, provide database and authentication services, deliver email or support security and maintenance. They may process data on our behalf under appropriate contractual or other safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the Platform, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer. We do not sell personal data.

7. International transfers

Some infrastructure and service providers may process or store data outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we will use the safeguards required by applicable data-protection law, including an adequate level of protection or another permitted transfer mechanism. Contact us if you need further information about relevant safeguards.

8. Retention

We keep account and contribution data while it is needed to provide the service or while content remains published. Routine activity and audit metadata is scheduled for deletion after 180 days. Records may be retained longer where reasonably necessary to investigate a security incident, resolve a dispute, comply with law or protect the rights of users. We periodically review whether retained data is still needed and delete or anonymise it when the purpose has ended.

9. Security

We use access controls, database policies, restricted administrator access and audit logging intended to protect personal data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will assess it and notify the Data Commissioner and affected individuals where and as required by Kenyan law.

10. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to be informed about processing, access your personal data, correct inaccurate data, object to processing, request erasure of data that should no longer be held, restrict processing or request portability where applicable. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain limited information where the law permits or requires it.

To exercise these rights, email jarkpruden@gmail.com with the subject “Arc Africa Data Request”, or use the Platform contact channel. You may also lodge a complaint with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The Platform uses browser storage and session mechanisms needed for authentication and core functionality. We do not describe these as advertising trackers. If optional analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, this Policy will be updated and any required choices will be provided.

12. Children

The Platform is designed for a general technology community and is not intended to collect children’s personal data without the required authorisation. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate authorisation, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when our processing changes or legal requirements evolve. The current version and effective date will appear here. Questions, privacy requests or complaints can be sent to jarkpruden@gmail.com.

This Policy is a practical disclosure for the current Platform features, not a legal compliance certification. Before launch, Arc Africa’s operator should confirm the legal controller identity, registration obligations, vendor agreements, international-transfer safeguards and whether a Data Protection Impact Assessment is required.
