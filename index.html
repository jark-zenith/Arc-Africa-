Our model

A community for building businesses, not applying for jobs.

Arc Africa is a venture-building branch of Pruden Technologies — not an employment agency.

Members should be able to meet other people with complementary skills, share or discover business ideas, discuss problems, form working teams and decide whether they want to collaborate, partner or co-found a venture. Ideas listed here are starting points, not the complete catalogue; new ideas can be proposed and added as the community grows. Nothing is automatically a job, funded project or formal partnership. No salary, equity, funding, contract or role is promised. Any ownership, compensation, responsibilities, intellectual property and decision rights must be agreed in writing by the people involved.

↔

Co-founders & venture partners

People interested in sharing responsibility, shaping strategy and helping build a venture over time. Any ownership, decision rights, time commitment and financial terms must be discussed and agreed in writing before commitments are made.

⌘

Builders & specialist collaborators

Developers, designers, engineers, researchers and operators who want to explore a specific problem, prototype or pilot. Scope, compensation if any, IP and deliverables must be agreed upfront.

◎

Advisers & ecosystem partners

Mentors, institutions, suppliers, licensed operators, customer-access partners and funders who can help validate an idea. Interest is exploratory until both sides confirm a concrete arrangement.

How Arc Africa works

From meeting people to co-founding ventures.

The aim is not to assign people to vacancies. It is to help people find each other, explore ideas, and voluntarily form teams around a shared opportunity.

01

Meet potential teammates

Introduce your skills, experience, interests, location and the kind of contribution you can realistically make.

02

Explore or propose ideas

Browse existing concepts or bring a new problem, business idea or opportunity for the community to discuss.

03

Choose to collaborate

Find people with complementary skills, discuss the problem and validate customer needs before deciding to proceed.

04

Agree on a venture

If the team chooses to move forward, document roles, ownership, time commitments, IP and financial terms before building or spending.

Team formation

Find the people the idea actually needs.

We do not need to recruit every specialist on day one. The right mix depends on which idea is validated, who commits, and what resources are available.

PHASE 01

Validate the problem

Customer interviews, field research, market sizing and testing assumptions before building too much.

PHASE 02

Form a core team

Agree on the problem, contribution, time commitment, decision-making and written collaboration terms.

PHASE 03

Build a small pilot

Test with real users or partners, track evidence, costs, risks and feedback.

PHASE 04

Earn the right to grow

Expand roles, spending and operations only when the pilot and resources justify it.

What we value:

Reliability, honest communication, learning in public, customer empathy, practical skills and willingness to solve hard problems together. You do not need to claim expertise you do not yet have; be clear about what you can contribute and what you want to learn.
