This is about partnership — not employment.

Arc Africa is not advertising jobs or promising salaries.

We are inviting expressions of interest from people who may want to explore co-founding, venture partnerships, technical collaboration, field research or early-stage project building. These ideas are at different stages of planning; they are not presented as funded or operating businesses. There is no guaranteed salary, equity, funding, contract or role from submitting this form.

Co-founders & venture partners

People interested in sharing responsibility, shaping strategy and helping build a venture over time. Any ownership, decision rights, time commitment and financial terms must be discussed and agreed in writing before commitments are made.

⌘

Builders & specialist collaborators

Developers, designers, engineers, researchers and operators who want to explore a specific problem, prototype or pilot. Scope, compensation if any, IP and deliverables must be agreed upfront.

◎

Advisers & ecosystem partners

Mentors, institutions, suppliers, licensed operators, customer-access partners and funders who can help validate an idea. Interest is exploratory until both sides confirm a concrete arrangement.

01 / AI Business Assistant

A mobile-first tool for shops, salons, garages, kiosks and small farms to track sales, stock, customer credit and basic accounts. Later versions could answer questions and flag stock or cash-flow issues in plain language.

Initial resourcesLower cost
Guide estimate to first customer2–4 months
Primary challengeAdoption & trust
First milestoneValidate with shops

First version to explore

  • Fast sale and stock recording
  • Customer credit and daily summary
  • Offline-friendly experience; English and Swahili
  • AI insights later, with answers grounded in real numbers

Potential early team

Product / venture lead: customer research, priorities and early sales
Full-stack developer: app, API and data model
UI/UX designer: simple screens and usability testing
Later collaborators: AI/data, offline sync, bookkeeping adviser and shop onboarding

Good fit for

Software developers, AI/data builders, product designers, small-business operators and people able to reach pilot shops.

02 / Solar & Battery Micro-grids

Reliable solar and battery systems for homes, shops and community sites, potentially paid through deposits and instalments. Smart monitoring, maintenance and fair payment terms would be central to the model.

Initial resourcesMedium–high
Guide estimate to first customer4–8 months
Primary challengeCapital & servicing
First milestoneSupplier + pilot plan

First version to explore

  • Choose a narrow customer segment and survey real demand
  • Find credible suppliers and confirm warranty terms
  • Plan a small, safe pilot with qualified installers

Potential early team

Venture / operations lead: suppliers, customers and partners
Qualified solar engineer or electrician: system design and safety
Field technicians: installation and maintenance for a pilot
Later collaborators: credit/collections, logistics, finance and software monitoring

Good fit for

Qualified energy professionals, field operators, supply-chain specialists, financing partners and software builders.

How the team grows

Start lean. Add roles when needed.

We do not need to recruit every specialist on day one. The right mix depends on which idea is validated, who commits, and what resources are available.

PHASE 01

Validate the problem

Customer interviews, field research, market sizing and testing assumptions before building too much.

PHASE 02

Form a core team

Agree on the problem, contribution, time commitment, decision-making and written collaboration terms.

PHASE 03

Build a small pilot

Test with real users or partners, track evidence, costs, risks and feedback.

PHASE 04

Earn the right to grow

Expand roles, spending and operations only when the pilot and resources justify it.

What we value:

Reliability, honest communication, learning in public, customer empathy, practical skills and willingness to solve hard problems together. You do not need to claim expertise you do not yet have; be clear about what you can contribute and what you want to learn.
